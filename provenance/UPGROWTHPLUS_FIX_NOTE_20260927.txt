UP-Growth+ correctness fix

Problem:
The original update_exact_utility() could accumulate partial transaction
utility even when the complete candidate itemset was not contained in a
transaction.

Observed Foodmart mismatches before fix:
T6  546 vs 550
T5  769 vs 781
T4  1903 vs 1947
T3  6309 vs 6610
T2  40485 vs 46665
T1  85034 vs 100723

Correction:
Reimplemented exact-utility verification so utility is accumulated only
when every item of the candidate itemset occurs in the transaction.

Validation after correction:
Foodmart T10-T1: 10/10 exact matches with PA-HUIM.
Mismatches: 0.
